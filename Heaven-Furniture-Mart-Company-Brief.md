**WHO THEY ARE**

Heaven Furniture Mart is one of Chattogram's leading bespoke furniture brands. They design and craft custom furniture — sofas, beds, dining sets, office pieces — built around what a customer actually wants, not pulled off a shelf. Their pitch to the market is simple: "Designed. Crafted. Customized." They want their landing page to feel like someone just walked into a luxury interior studio — not an online furniture shop.

**WHAT THEY SELL**

* Living Room — sofas, coffee tables, TV units, consoles

* Bedroom — beds, wardrobes, dressing tables, bedside tables

* Dining — dining tables, dining chairs, cabinets

* Office & Study — executive tables, bookshelves, workstations

* Bespoke / Custom — anything built to a customer's own space, size, and taste

**THE BRAND VIBE**

In the client's own words: this should feel like someone "entered a luxury furniture and interior studio," not an online furniture shop.

**Go for:**

Warm, editorial, spacious, confident — real photography doing the talking.

**Avoid:**

Cheap, crowded, marketplace-y, generic template, loud colors, walls of text.

**WHAT YOUR LANDING PAGE ACTUALLY NEEDS**

These are **suggestions, not mandatory sections**. You can add pages and other sections that make the overall website look good.

1. **Hero —** One strong image/line that says who they are in one breath — e.g. "Furniture, Crafted Around You" \+ one clear button.

2. **Brand Intro —** 2–3 sentences: bespoke furniture \+ interior styling, from Chattogram.

3. **Why Choose Heaven —** The trust bullets above, laid out fast to scan.

4. **Collections Snapshot —** A few categories with photos — Living, Bedroom, Dining, Bespoke. Not a full catalog.

5. **Bespoke Highlight —** Give this its own moment. It's their \#1 differentiator.

6. **Social Proof —** The MD quote, or "hundreds of happy homeowners" \+ a showroom photo.

7. **One Clear Call-To-Action —** "Request a Quote" or "WhatsApp Us" — repeated, not competing with five other buttons.

8. **Footer —** Address, phone, socials.

**WHAT JUDGES ARE ACTUALLY SCORING**

* Does this feel like luxury, not a generic furniture shop? (the biggest single factor)

* Is the brand clear in the first few seconds?

* Does it look great on mobile?

* Does it push toward one clear action?

* Is it clean and fast — not cluttered, not slow?
